# DecisionOS

## Tagline

Analyze. Predict. Decide.

---

# Vision

DecisionOS is an AI-powered Decision Intelligence Platform that helps governments, organizations, and communities transform fragmented data into actionable decisions using AI.

Instead of simply displaying dashboards, DecisionOS analyzes information, predicts outcomes, recommends actions, and generates decision reports.

---

# Problem Statement

Communities generate huge amounts of information every day:

- Traffic
- Air Quality
- Weather
- Citizen Complaints
- Emergency Incidents
- Public Documents
- Utility Data

This information exists in different systems.

Decision makers spend too much time gathering information instead of making decisions.

DecisionOS solves this problem by bringing everything into one intelligent platform.

---

# Solution

DecisionOS combines:

- AI Decision Engine
- Predictive Analytics
- Document Intelligence (RAG)
- Vision AI
- AI Copilot
- Decision Reports

into one workspace.
